## Engineering Roles to Name in the Proposal

- **Mechanical**: Frame, cylinder, pressing barrel, guarding, layout, foundation loads, and maintenance access
- **Hydraulic**: Pump, valves, pressure settings, circuit, leakage criteria, oil specification, and spare seals
- **Electrical and Controls**: Voltage, frequency, motor, cabinet, PLC or relay logic, sensors, alarms, interlocks, and emergency stop
- **Process**: Raw-material preparation, batch load, pressure recipe, cycle, filtration, cleaning, product and cake handling
- **Quality**: Inspection plan, material records, calibration, factory tests, nonconformance, corrective action, and final document pack

The quotation or technical agreement should name the responsible reviewer or department and the documents each role must approve.

## Export Coordination

Confirm responsibility for the commercial invoice, packing list, HS code, certificate of origin where included, inspection certificate where required, manuals, drawings, destination-language labels, voltage, plugs, export packing, container loading, Incoterm, freight, insurance, and customs documents.

## Installation and After-Sales

Define whether installation, commissioning, and training are remote or on site. State service hours, support channels, response targets, travel, visas, accommodation, labor, spare parts, consumables, warranty, maintenance, and exclusions in the contract. Ongoing technical support may be provided within the agreed arrangement.

## Before Requesting a Quote

Provide the material, representative sample, target product, planned hours, destination, utilities, line scope, acceptance tests, and required documents.
