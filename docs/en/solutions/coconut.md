# Coconut Oil Production Line

This page is for a fresh mature coconut project that may produce coconut water, coconut milk, desiccated coconut, crude coconut oil, refined coconut oil, and packaged finished products. The line scope is different from a simple hydraulic batch-press quotation for dry copra. Before equipment is fixed, confirm which commercial outputs the factory will actually sell.

![Prepared coconut material for route selection](/images/coconut-puree-e2083a9300.webp)

## Full-Line Scope

The complete route combines dehusking, coconut conveying, manual shell removal, coconut water filtration and storage, meat washing, meat crushing, hydraulic milk pressing, drying, mixing, screw oil pressing, centrifugal separation, optional refining, and final filling, capping, labeling, and coding.

## Product Routes

| Route | Commercial outputs | Main operations | Key confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wet processing | Coconut water and coconut milk | Collect and filter coconut water; wash and crush meat; press coconut milk with hydraulic presses; store or fill liquid products | Hygiene level, storage volume, filtration media, milk extraction basis, cleaning method |
| Drying and oil extraction | Desiccated coconut, crude oil, refined oil, packed oil | Dry pressed coconut material; mix and elevate; press oil with screw presses; separate, store, refine if required, and pack | Inlet/outlet moisture, oil-yield basis, oil temperature, sediment, refining stages, packaging format |

## Factory Process Flow

1. Dehusk, convey, and remove coconut shell.
2. Collect, filter, and store coconut water.
3. Wash, crush, and hydraulically press coconut meat for milk.
4. Dry, mix, elevate, and screw press the remaining coconut solids.
5. Separate, store, and refine coconut oil as required.
6. Fill, cap, label, and code the selected finished products.

## Data Required for a Quotation

- Product plan: coconut water, coconut milk, desiccated coconut, crude oil, refined oil, or packed oil
- Capacity basis: whole coconuts, coconut meat, coconut milk, dried coconut, crude oil, or finished oil
- Working hours, raw coconut size range, local labor plan, and automation expectation
- Moisture targets before drying and before screw pressing
- Required oil quality, aroma, clarity, sediment limit, water limit, and refining stages
- Bottle type, cap type, label type, coding content, and final pack format
- Power, heat source, clean water, wastewater plan, drainage, ventilation, floor space, destination, and delivery terms

## Acceptance Records

For FAT or SAT, record the input basis, rejected coconuts, water recovery, meat washing result, crushed particle size, hydraulic press charge, milk output, dryer inlet/outlet moisture, screw-press feed rate, crude oil, separated water and solids, refined oil, packaging accuracy, and cleaning of coconut-contact surfaces.
