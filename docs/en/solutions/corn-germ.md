# Corn Germ Oil Processing Line

Corn germ from wet milling, dry milling, or another separation process can differ in moisture, starch, bran, and particle structure. The quotation must identify the germ source and condition before selecting drying, flaking, conditioning, and extraction equipment.

![Corn germ preparation before oil extraction](/images/hot-pressing-process-of-corn-germ-2dad9e44c2.webp)

## Raw Material Boundary

State wet or dry germ, milling process, moisture, starch and bran carryover, impurities, storage time, and available oil analysis. Wet germ must be stabilized or dried promptly. Quote capacity on clean prepared germ and record any receiving or drying loss separately.

## Recommended Pretreatment

1. Receive, weigh, and clean the germ.
2. Stabilize and dry wet germ under controlled conditions.
3. Cool and store only after the moisture condition is suitable.
4. Crack, grind, or flake according to germ structure.
5. Condition heat and moisture for the selected pressing route.

## Pressing or Dedicated Route

For commercial duty, compare continuous screw pressing, pre-pressing, or another dedicated extraction route according to germ condition, throughput, oil target, and meal use. A hydraulic batch trial can compare low-temperature and conditioned samples but should not define plant capacity without measured cycle and mass-balance data.

## Filtration and Finished-Oil Target

Define crude filtered or refined corn germ oil, solids, moisture, color, odor, degumming and refining scope, and required laboratory tests. State whether meal or cake quality and residual oil are part of acceptance.

## Data Required for a Quotation

- Wet- or dry-milling germ sample, moisture, starch and bran contamination, impurities, storage time, and available oil analysis
- Clean-germ input per hour or day, shifts, seasonal variation, and receiving or drying schedule
- Stabilization, drying, grinding or flaking, conditioning, and selected pressing or extraction study
- Crude or refined oil target, filtration and refining scope, meal or cake use, tanks, and packaging
- Steam or heat, power, water, conveying, floor space, destination, and delivery terms

## Acceptance Records

Record receiving and prepared-germ weights, rejected impurities, moisture before and after stabilization or drying, grind or flake condition, conditioning settings, feed rate or batch charge, crude and filtered oil, and meal or cake. Document filter loading, oil sampling points, residual-oil test method, cleaning, and the exact germ lot used for any capacity comparison.
