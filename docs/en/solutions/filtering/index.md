# Liquid filtration systems before filling

![Liquid filtration system](/images/filling-solutions/filtering.webp)

Filtration prepares a liquid for storage, further processing or filling by removing the agreed type and amount of suspended material. In an edible-oil project it can sit after pressing or settling; in a beverage or dairy project it must follow the approved product process. The required endpoint must be defined by the buyer rather than described only as clear liquid.

## Position in the process

A common edible-oil route is:

**Press discharge -> settling or buffer -> filtration -> clean-oil tank -> filling**

Other liquids may require filtration before heat treatment, after clarification or immediately before the filler. The equipment proposal should state the exact location, allowable inlet condition and downstream cleanliness target.

## Product and separation basis

The supplier needs to understand:

- Liquid identity, temperature, viscosity and food or chemical duty
- Expected solids type, concentration and particle behavior
- Required filtrate appearance or analytical endpoint
- Batch or continuous operating basis
- Cleaning method and allowable product loss
- Filter-cake, sludge or spent-media handling
- Product-contact material and pressure or vacuum limitations specified by the process owner

## Procurement decisions that affect price

- Coarse protection, clarification, polishing or staged filtration duty
- Filter media type and whether consumables are included
- Manual cleaning, replaceable media or automated cleaning expectation
- Feed tank, pump, valves, piping and instrumentation boundary
- Mobile skid, stand-alone machine or fixed line installation
- Connection to storage, refining or filling equipment
- Destination utilities, layout, packing and service scope

## Information needed for a quote

Provide a representative product description and sample data, solids condition, required filtrate result, operating temperature, target throughput basis, cleaning procedure, upstream and downstream equipment, utilities, layout and destination. If a filtration trial is requested, agree sample volume, media, test endpoint, shipping and disposal responsibility first.

## Acceptance and after-sales boundary

FAT should use the approved liquid or a documented substitute and a written method for evaluating filtrate, flow stability, leakage, cleaning and residual product. Consumables, replacement-media prices, local piping, installation, travel, commissioning, operator training and recommended spare parts must be itemized. No filtration result or service item should be assumed beyond the signed technical and commercial scope.
