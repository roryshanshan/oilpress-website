# Oil Production Line Builder

Choose your oil and pressing route, tick the optional machines you want, and send the list to us for a quotation. Required machines are ticked for you; every linked machine opens its own page.

<LineBuilder />

## What happens after you send the list

1. Our engineer checks the machines against your seed, route and daily tonnage, and sets how many of each machine you need.
2. We send a quotation with models, power, floor space and delivery time for the whole line.
3. If your seed is unusual, we ask for a sample and run a pressing test in our factory before you order.
