# Automatic pneumatic capping machine

![Automatic pneumatic capping machine](/images/filling-products/pneumatic-capping-machine.webp)

This page covers a capping station whose working stroke is driven by compressed air. “Pneumatic” identifies the actuation method, not the closure geometry: the exact pressing-head form and the applicable cap type must be confirmed from the actual bottle and cap samples before the machine is configured.

## Application and selection boundary

- Product family: [Sealing Machine Series](/en/solutions/sealing/)
- Published parameter reference: [Filling and packaging equipment parameter library](/en/products/filling-equipment)
- Use in an automatic line depends on the target cap, bottle support method, cap-feeding arrangement and required cycle rate.
- Compatibility with a twist-off aluminum cap, a press-on closure or another cap cannot be concluded from the machine name or cap diameter alone.

## Compressed-air requirements

Ask the supplier to state the required pressure range at the machine inlet while cycling, the average and peak air flow or consumption at the target rate, and the air connection. Also confirm the required filtration, dryness and oil condition, plus whether the filter-regulator, lubricator if specified, receiver or low-pressure monitoring is included. Compressor nameplate pressure alone is not enough if the line has pressure drop under load.

## Cylinder, pressing head and positioning

- Cylinder bore, stroke, available pressing force, cushioning, dwell and return settings should be selected from the sample package and the required cycle.
- Confirm the pressing head's exact geometry, contact material, cap holding or release method and allowable contact area by sample test. Do not assume that the machine uses a generic flat head, a profiled head or a rolling head.
- The compatible closure type must be stated in the quotation after sample review. A head made for one cap profile may not locate or load another profile correctly.
- Confirm bottle centering, neck or base support, cap centering before the downstroke, height adjustment and the scope of cap-presence or misalignment detection.

## Cycle rate and changeover

The quoted rate should identify the bottle and cap format, cap-feeding condition, pressing dwell and compressed-air conditions used for the calculation or trial. Validate the rate in a sustained sample run, including cylinder recovery and upstream or downstream handoff.

For each additional format, list the pressing head, bottle guide or holder, height and pressure settings, sensors and cap-feed change parts. Changeover time and the settings needed to reproduce an accepted result should be agreed rather than described only as “quick.”

## Safeguarding and acceptance

- Define the supplied guarding around the descending head and cylinder, interlocked access where required, emergency stop arrangement, and a means to isolate and exhaust residual air during setup or maintenance.
- Acceptance checks should cover cap seating height and level, visible cap or bottle damage, retention or opening force as applicable, leakage where the closure provides a seal, and the sustained cycle rate under the agreed air supply.
- Safety functions and performance limits should be verified against the final machine configuration; they should not be treated as unconditional claims.
