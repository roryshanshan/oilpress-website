## Single-head operating scope

This machine shrinks PVC capsules on the bottle mouth after corking; it is not the cap-rolling model. The published envelope is bottle height 180-350, bottle diameter 60-100, capsule length 30-70, and capsule diameter 30-35. Final purchasing still requires representative bottles and capsules, and the page does not state whether capsule placement is manual or automatic.

For an inline project, first match the discharge rhythm from the [Corking Machine Series](/en/solutions/corking/), then check the handoff to the [Labeling Machine Series](/en/solutions/labeling/). For stand-alone use, describe the intended infeed and outfeed in the inquiry. "Single-head" does not mean manual operation and does not establish that conveyors or capsule application are included.

No standard steam, electric-heating, or hot-air configuration is named here, and no temperature or heating construction is published. The supplier's technical quotation must identify the actual method and site interface without adding an assumed operating principle.

## Samples and quote scope

A purchase inquiry should state target rate, operating mode, bottle and capsule dimensions, capsule material, upstream corking and capsule-placement method, and downstream labeling interface. Supply representative samples or drawings. A lower-speed application still needs sample confirmation.

Ask the manufacturer to separate the base-machine price, supplied heat source, conveyor scope, format parts for each bottle/capsule, controls, spares, packing, and optional services. The public dimensional envelope is a selection range, not proof that guides, locating parts, or other adjustments are unnecessary; the delivered parts list controls.

## Single-head FAT/SAT

At FAT, run each contracted format with the approved samples and document changeover, single-head infeed/outfeed, capsule appearance, and the agreed rate. Explicitly exclude capsule feeders, conveyors, or signals that are outside the quotation.

At SAT, verify the actual site power or other contracted heating utility, upstream/downstream height and pace, agreed continuous-run condition, and after-sales handover documents. Public output figures do not replace a signed test duration and acceptance standard.
