# Multi-head rubber cap heat shrink machine

![Multi-head cap shrinking machine](/images/filling-products/multi-head-cap-shrinking-machine.webp)

Head count and line balance define this page. In the published comparison, RS-1 has one head and a listed output of 2000 p/h, while RS-6 has six heads and 6000 p/h. The multi-head purchase case should therefore be tied to the RS-6 row; RS-1 remains in the table as the single-head comparison, not as a second multi-head configuration.

## Multi-head rhythm and line balance

- **Model-to-rate match:** the six-head row carries the higher published output. A quotation should bind model, head count, and required line rate instead of stating only "multi-head" or the full series range.
- **Bottle and capsule scope:** the public range is bottle height 180-350, bottle diameter 60-100, PVC capsule length 30-70, and capsule diameter 30-35. Confirm units and sample fit in the technical agreement; a range does not mean every format uses one tooling set.
- **Function boundary:** this page covers heat shrinking, not cap rolling. When rolling or bottle-mouth finishing is required, compare the [Cap Rolling and Shrinking Machine](/en/products/filling/cap-rolling-shrinking-machine) and put the extra assembly in writing.
- **Heat-source boundary:** the page does not specify steam, electric heating, or hot air, and it does not publish a heating principle. State the supplied method and utility interface in the purchase specification.
- **Upstream and downstream pace:** place the station after the [Corking Machine Series](/en/solutions/corking/) and capsule application, then before the [Labeling Machine Series](/en/solutions/labeling/). The public page does not say that a capsule feeder, buffer, reject device, or particular line signals are included.

## Multi-head quotation and tooling list

Give the supplier the target output, existing conveyor rate, bottle and capsule samples, dimensioned drawings, capsule material, corker details, capsule-placement method, and downstream labeler information. If only drawings are available, the quote should name the later sample-approval point.

The commercial offer should itemize the selected model and head count, actual heating configuration, quantity of format parts for each bottle/capsule, whether each head needs a matched set, conveyor and control interfaces, spares, export packing, and after-sales scope. It should also say whether capsule feeding or placement is included, so the shrinker price is not mistaken for the price of the complete bottle-neck packaging section.

## FAT/SAT across supplied heads

- Use approved sample bottles and capsules at FAT and cover every supplied head and every contracted format.
- Compare the finish from each head with the approved sample, and record infeed/outfeed transfer, the agreed test rate, restart behavior, and purchased signals. Do not turn an uncontracted feature into a performance promise.
- At SAT, reconnect the actual corking, capsule-placement, and labeling equipment and verify the written rate, utility, and changeover conditions. Test duration and acceptance limits should be agreed before shipment.
