## Validate the 36H application first

Low-vacuum constant-level filling depends on a repeatable seal at a rigid bottle neck and on a still liquid that can circulate without unacceptable foam. Test the production recipe at normal and boundary temperatures. Wine, rice wine, vinegar, soy sauce and comparable non-carbonated liquids can enter the evaluation, but suitability remains tied to the supplied samples.

Sparkling liquids, products with suspended solids, paste-like viscosity, collapsible containers and legal quantity control by mass fall outside the normal selection basis. They should trigger a review of counter-pressure, large-port, positive-displacement or weighing equipment as appropriate.

## Model-specific purchasing checks

- Ask for the connected-load list and operating utility assumptions, including electrical supply, vacuum arrangement, product feed and any compressed-air demand.
- Map the space for bottle accumulation, operator access, cleaning hoses and removal of guards before approving the 2500 x 2350 mm machine position.
- Require a line responsibility matrix showing which supplier owns the rinser-filler and filler-closure interfaces.

The 36-head count and 10,000-bottle reference are published selection data, not evidence of line availability, product accuracy or container-loss performance.

## Line interfaces and changeover plan

For a 10,000 bottles/hour reference project, define upstream product availability and downstream accumulation in minutes or bottle count. The controls should exchange ready, run, fault and blocked/starved states with adjoining equipment.

At this reference rate, issue a responsibility matrix for process supply, bottle handling and controls. Specify product feed stability, return and drain points; rinser discharge and conveyor elevation; closure, inspection and labeling readiness; accumulation measured in bottles; and blocked/starved communication. Include utility connection loads in the interface register.

Cleaning must be designed around the actual recipe sequence. Obtain wetted-part materials, elastomer compatibility, circuit volume, low points, dismantling tools and the boundary of any circulation cleaning. Define format storage and verification after reassembly.

## Samples and manufacturer quote inputs

A 36H quotation package should include representative liquid and bottles, viscosity and temperature bands, foam observation, target level, neck finish, closure, required accepted rate, accumulation strategy, plant layout, utility quality, controls protocol, country of installation, documentation language and the requested site-service tasks.

## FAT, SAT and acceptance basis

Include planned starved and blocked conditions in the acceptance run. Confirm that restart does not create an unagreed wave of underfilled, overfilled or foaming bottles, and define how those bottles are identified.

Introduce controlled starved and blocked states during FAT, then measure recovery as well as steady running. State how many acceptable bottles must be produced, the observation interval, allowed level range, foam or overflow limit and reject classification. Repeat critical interface cases during SAT with the actual site utilities.
