# Rotary bottle rinser

![Rotary bottle washing machine](/images/filling-products/rotary-bottle-washer.webp)

The source description for the rotary QSP-6A gives a specific water sequence: front-side bottle loading and unloading, a six-wheel drive, three water distributors, a recirculation tank and pump, two recirculated-water rinses, and one clean-water final rinse. Drive speed is steplessly adjustable. In this description, "six-wheel drive" is a feature of the rotary machine and must not be confused with the separate six-wheel washer family.

## Rotary sequence and process limits

Bottles move through the recirculated-water and clean-water positions in the machine's rotary rhythm, so infeed transfer, internal movement, and discharge must remain synchronized. Recirculated water serves the first two rinses and clean water the last. The source does not specify filtration grade, make-up control, blowdown interval, nozzle count, bottle inversion, brushes, or chemical dosing. Those items exist only if they appear in the accepted quotation and water schematic.

The table supports initial selection of regular bottles. Irregular containers, lightweight PET, or bottles with an offset center of gravity require sample testing because rotary transfer is sensitive to repeatable bottle positioning.

## Samples, changeovers and adjacent machines

The buyer supplies physical samples for every bottle family, including dimensional extremes and the lightest or least stable format. The manufacturer uses them to confirm guides, transfer parts, pitch, operating range, and changeover steps. Because QSP-6A also appears in the six-wheel washer table, purchase documents should always state "rotary bottle rinser" together with this page's capacity, power, and dimensions instead of relying on the model code alone.

The upstream conveyor needs controlled bottle flow and appropriate accumulation. Downstream equipment must receive bottles after the clean-water rinse and provide any required drainage, air drying, filling, capping, or labeling. Conveyor elevation, direction, speed reference, interlocks, and jam handling are set in the approved line layout.

## Water system, drainage and layout

The table lists 1.5 m3/h water consumption and a rinse-pressure value of 0.1-0.25, but no pressure unit is shown. The quotation must add the unit, measurement point, and permitted variation. The buyer supplies clean water of the agreed quality and pressure, power, and external drainage to the defined boundary.

Although the source expressly mentions the recirculation tank and pump, the status of filters, make-up valve, overflow, blowdown, and wastewater treatment must still be itemized. If hot water, detergent, or disinfectant will be used in either circuit, the buyer provides composition, concentration, temperature, safety data, and discharge conditions; the manufacturer reviews wetted materials and seals before approval.

The published footprint excludes service access to the tank, pump, drive, and controls, as well as conveyor transitions and floor drainage. Final placement follows an agreed interface drawing.
