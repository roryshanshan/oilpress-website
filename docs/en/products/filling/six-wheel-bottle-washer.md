# Six-wheel rinsing machine

![Six-wheel-bottle-washer](/images/filling-products/six-wheel-bottle-washer.webp)

The six-wheel washer uses a sleeve roller chain to carry bottles through its rinse zones. Recirculated water is applied by both tracking sprays and fixed-pipe sprays, while the clean-water section uses a distributor that follows the bottle position. That combination, rather than the model code alone, distinguishes this family from a basic chain-rail washer and from the rotary QSP-6A.

## Chain transport and spray arrangement

The roller chain establishes bottle pitch through the machine. Tracking sprays must remain synchronized with that pitch; fixed piping covers a defined section of travel, and the clean-water distributor supplies the final tracking spray. A continuous chain path needs stable support and coordinated infeed and discharge so that bottles do not crowd at the entrance or accumulate at the exit.

The source does not define inversion, brushes, nozzle count, or a cleaning-result class. It also does not say which tanks, pumps, filters, make-up controls, overflow, blowdown, and connecting pipes are included. The water-system boundary therefore has to be itemized even though recirculated and clean-water stages are both described.

## Glass and PET formats

The table covers glass or PET bottles from 50-100 mm in diameter and 100-320 mm in height. Bottles within that envelope can still differ in shoulder profile, base geometry, stiffness, and center of gravity, requiring dedicated guides or supports. Lightweight PET should be trialled to confirm that bottle handling and spray force do not destabilize it.

## Price, test plan and support

QSP-6A and QSP-6B cannot be priced from output alone. Bottle parts, tracking-spray execution, water-system scope, controls, adjacent conveyors, spares, export packing, installation, and commissioning all affect the purchase price. The offer should list the main machine, format parts, distributors and water circuit, controls, tools, documentation, FAT, packing, and buyer-supplied items.

If cleaning performance is to be quantified, the contamination sample and inspection method must be agreed before testing rather than replaced by an unsupported cleanliness claim.
