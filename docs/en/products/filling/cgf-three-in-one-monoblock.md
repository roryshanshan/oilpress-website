# CGF Rinsing, Filling and Capping Monoblock

![CGF Rinsing, Filling and Capping Monoblock](/images/filling-products/cgf-three-in-one-monoblock.webp)

The CGF monoblock places bottle rinsing, quantitative filling and capping on one coordinated rotary platform. It can simplify transfer between these three stages, but every proposal must be matched to the product, rigid bottle, closure and sanitation method used by the buyer.

## Application and purchasing fit

- Product family: [Filling and packaging line overview](/en/solutions/filling-packages)
- Parameter reference: [Filling and packaging equipment parameter library](/en/products/filling-equipment)
- Choose the head combination only after confirming the required sustained output for the actual bottle and fill volume.
- Define the rinse medium and drainage arrangement, the filling method and the cap-feeding and closing process.
- Check access for cleaning, format change and maintenance as part of the layout review.

## What to provide for a manufacturer quotation

- Product properties, fill-volume range, bottle and cap drawings or samples, and required contact materials.
- Rinse specification, water or product recovery expectations, cleaning method and drainage conditions.
- Required output by format, upstream bottle supply, downstream inspection and accumulation requirements.
- Site utilities, floor plan, FAT samples, acceptance limits and commissioning or training requested in the offer.

Use representative samples for the factory acceptance test whenever practical. The agreed test material, run duration, sampling method and pass limits should be written into the purchase specification.

## Price, delivery, and written service scope

Manufacturer pricing depends on the confirmed machine configuration, format parts, controls, guarding, line interfaces, utilities and destination. Delivery schedule, export packing, installation, commissioning, training, spare parts and after-sales support apply only as stated in the written quotation and contract.
