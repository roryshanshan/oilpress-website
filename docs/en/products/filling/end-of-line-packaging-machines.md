# End-of-line packaging machines for edible oil

![Plastic drum line: case packing, shrink wrapping, palletizing and pallet wrapping](/images/end-of-line/oil-drum-case-packing-shrink-palletizing-wrapping-line.webp)

After filling, capping and labeling, oil bottles and jugs still need to be boxed, sealed, stacked and wrapped for shipping. This page lists the end-of-line machines we supply with our filling lines: case erectors, case packers, case sealers, shrink wrappers, strapping machines, palletizers, pallet wrappers and conveyors. They are built by our packaging-machine partner and quoted, shipped and commissioned together with the filling line.

The values below are the published figures for each machine. The real rate depends on your bottles, cartons and pack pattern. The full English catalog with photos is also on our main site: [end-of-line packaging machines](https://hydraulic-oil-press.com/products/filling-machine-series/end-of-line-packaging-machines/).

## Line layout

**Filled and capped bottles → case erecting → case packing → carton sealing → shrink wrapping (optional) → strapping (optional) → palletizing → pallet wrapping**

Shrink wrapping and strapping depend on how you sell: film multipacks for supermarkets, cartons for distributors, or wrapped pallets for export.

## Plastic drum line: case packing, shrink wrapping, palletizing and pallet wrapping

For 5 L, 10 L and 20 L plastic or metal oil drums, one line packs the drums into cartons, shrink-wraps, palletizes and stretch-wraps the pallet. The grippers are pneumatic so the drums do not dent.

**Drum sizes**: 5 L, 10 L, 20 L · **Speed**: up to 60 drums/min · **Options**: Weighing, labeling

## Which machines do you need?

- **Small bottling room:** a semi-automatic case erector and case sealer, with cartons packed by hand.
- **Bottled-oil brand, up to about 12 cartons per minute:** automatic case erector, a drop or pick-and-place case packer, a flap folding sealer and a turntable pallet wrapper.
- **Supermarket multipacks:** add a sleeve shrink wrapper to group 2, 3 or 4 rows of bottles in film.
- **Export or high-volume lines:** add a palletizer, pallet conveyors and an in-line pallet wrapper.
- **Jugs and drums:** a drum case packer or the complete drum packing line above.

For a layout, send the bottle or jug size, bottles per carton, carton size, output per minute, pallet size and voltage.
