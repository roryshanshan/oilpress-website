## Hot-Press Trial and Acceptance Record

Oil recovery is not a fixed model value. For 300/325 hot-press projects, compare repeated trials on the same raw-material lot and record the heating step instead of using a generic yield table.

| Trial field | Record before acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Raw material | Variety, supplier lot, cleaned feed mass, impurity level and measured moisture |
| Preparation | Crushing size, roasting or conditioning temperature, holding time and feed temperature |
| Press cycle | Basket size, actual load, pressure sequence, holding time, unloading time and complete cycle time |
| Oil result | Net oil after the agreed settling or filtration step, with container tare removed |
| Cake result | Cake mass and residual oil measured by the agreed laboratory method |
| Comparison basis | At least two repeatable runs using the same lot, calculation formula and measuring instruments |

> Feed-basis yield and recovery relative to measured oil content are different figures. The quotation and acceptance document should state which calculation is used; no percentage is guaranteed without a representative witnessed trial.

## Pressing Material Guide:
+ Partial oil material pressing process guidance as follows:
+ Peanut (Recommended 300AC-390)
        + Peanut oil (rich aroma type) hot pressing process: Crush peanuts -> roast the peanut granules in a flat-bottom wok -> feed them into the hydraulic oil press barrel for pressing.
        + Record roasting temperature and time, feed temperature, oil color and aroma, net filtered oil and cake residual oil for the buyer's peanut lot.
        + Determine settling and filtration from the required finished-oil specification; no universal filtration exemption or unsupported brand reference is assumed.
        + ![Peanut hot pressing process](/images/process-image-48001f0b27.webp)
    + Sesame (Recommended 300AC-390)
        + Sesame hot-press route: clean and roast the sesame, then press it directly. Record the roast profile, oil temperature, net filtered oil, sediment and cake residual oil.
        + Determine settling and filtration from the required finished-oil specification; no universal filtration exemption or unsupported brand reference is assumed.
        + ![Sesame hot pressing process](/images/process-image-dfc1cdf2c9.webp)

        + Tea seed oil hot pressing process: Tea seeds go through screening, cleaning, drying, crushing, steaming and stir-frying, then connected to hydraulic oil press barrel pressing.
