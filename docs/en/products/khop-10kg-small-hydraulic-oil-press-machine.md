# KHOP-10KG Small Hydraulic Oil Press Machine

![KHOP-10KG small hydraulic oil press machine](/images/korean-type-small-press/khop-10kg.webp)

The KHOP-10KG is a small-batch hydraulic press configured for sesame-oil shops, fresh-oil counters, and sample-led premium-oil projects. Its hydraulic cycle and shop-facing layout can reduce some manual pressing steps, while feeding, loading, cake removal, cleaning, and material handling depend on the ordered configuration.

## Key advantages

- Hydraulic cylinder and small-batch loading arrangement
- Configurable hydraulic cycle and control interface
- Visible pressing area for process observation
- Compact structure for shops and retail counters
- Material suitability confirmed by a representative sample trial

### Reference capacity and acceptance boundary

The `1.0`, `1.8`, and `2.1 t/24 h` figures shown on this page are supplier reference values from specific test conditions, not guaranteed output. Before quotation and contract, record the raw-material species and lot, pretreatment and particle-size distribution, feed moisture, batch mass, and the complete cycle from loading through pressing, oil drainage, cake removal, cleaning, and restart. The FAT/SAT protocol must also define the continuous test duration, permitted stops, weighing and calibration method, sampling or laboratory method, acceptance tolerance, utilities, and operator actions included. Only criteria written into the signed contract are binding.

“Automatic hydraulic press” in the table describes the hydraulic-cycle and control configuration; it does not mean that feeding, unloading, cleaning, or all material handling are unattended.
