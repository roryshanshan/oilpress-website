![Dry peanut peeling machine with stainless hopper and skin fan](/images/peanut-peeling/dry-peanut-peeling-machine.webp)

## How It Works

![Two peelers with painted frames and rubbing roller beds](/images/peanut-peeling/dry-peanut-peeling-machine-painted-frame.webp)

- **Rubbing:** kernels drop from the hopper onto flexible rubber rollers that rub the brittle roasted skin loose without cracking the kernel. The gap adjusts to kernel size.
- **Separating:** a fan draws the loose skins away into a collection bag; peeled kernels run out of the discharge chute. No hand sieving.
- **One operator:** start motor and fan, tip kernels into the hopper, change the skin bag when full.

## Before You Order

- **Roast first.** This is a dry peeler for roasted or fried kernels. Raw peanuts need a wet (soak) peeler.
- **Feed warm, not hot.** Kernels straight from the roaster are soft and break; cool them on a tray first.
- **Peel for butter and food, not for oil.** An oil press does not need peeled kernels. Peel for peanut butter, peanut candy, fried or chopped peanuts and peanut milk.
- **Match it to the mill.** 200 kg/h is about four times what one 50 kg/h butter mill grinds, so one peeler can feed several mills.
