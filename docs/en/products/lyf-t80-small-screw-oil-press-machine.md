### Capacity and acceptance boundary

The throughput figures on this page are supplier reference values from specific test conditions; they are not guaranteed output and do not constitute an acceptance criterion. Before quotation and contract, record the raw-material species and lot, pretreatment and particle size, feed moisture, batch mass, and the complete cycle from feeding through pressing, oil drainage, cake discharge, cleaning and restart. The FAT/SAT protocol must also define the continuous test duration, permitted stops, weighing and calibration method, sampling or laboratory method, acceptance tolerance, utilities and operator actions included. Only criteria written into the signed contract are binding.

“Automatic” in the table describes the pressing and heating control configuration. It does not mean that feeding, cake removal, cleaning or all material handling are unattended.

## Oilseeds this size of press is used for

Presses of this size are commonly used for:

- Peanut kernel
- Sesame
- Rapeseed
- Sunflower seed
- Flaxseed
- Walnut kernel
- Camellia / tea seed
- Coconut

Confirm suitability with a representative sample trial before ordering. Very hard, very fibrous or very low-oil material may not be workable on a bench-top press.
