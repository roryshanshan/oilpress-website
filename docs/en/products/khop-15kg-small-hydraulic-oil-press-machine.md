### Reference capacity and acceptance boundary

The `1.0` and `1.8 t/24 h` figures shown on this page are supplier reference values from specific test conditions, not guaranteed output. Before quotation and contract, record the raw-material species and lot, pretreatment and particle-size distribution, feed moisture, batch mass, and the complete cycle from loading through pressing, oil drainage, cake removal, cleaning, and restart. The FAT/SAT protocol must also define the continuous test duration, permitted stops, weighing and calibration method, sampling or laboratory method, acceptance tolerance, utilities, and operator actions included. Only criteria written into the signed contract are binding.

“Automatic hydraulic press” describes the hydraulic-cycle and control configuration; feeding, loading, cake removal, cleaning, and material handling may still require operators.
