# Small Sesame Oil Press Machine

![Small sesame oil press machine](/images/korean-type-small-press/small-sesame-hero.webp)

The small sesame oil press is a small-batch equipment category commonly considered for sesame-oil shops, organic-oil counters, and compact commercial rooms. Most models on this page use a Korean-type hydraulic layout; final oil quality and recovery depend on the raw material, pretreatment, operating window, and agreed test method.

## Why it is often shortlisted for sesame

Small Korean-type hydraulic presses use a batch loading and pressing layout that can be evaluated with sesame samples before purchase. Relevant selection points include:

- Batch size and basket geometry for shop handling
- Pressure, hold-time, and oil-drain settings available for trial records
- Low-temperature processing only when the measured material temperature meets the agreed limit
- A visible process layout for storefront production

That is why this type of machine is commonly recommended for sesame-focused retail businesses rather than general large-scale oilseed projects.

## Small sesame press vs industrial hydraulic press

| Item | Small Sesame Press | Industrial Hydraulic Press |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capacity | 5-20 kg | 60-100 kg |
| Typical material focus | Sesame | Multi oil seeds |
| Usage | Shop / Retail | Factory |
| Production mode | Small batch | Bulk production |
| Investment | Low | Medium to high |

## When this machine is not the first choice

Although the machine can technically handle some other premium materials, it is not usually selected for heavy-duty bulk processing projects such as:

- Large peanut oil output
- Soybean industrial pressing
- Rapeseed or mustard bulk production
- Large flaxseed throughput
- Factory-scale multi-seed projects

### Capacity and acceptance boundary

The `1.0`, `1.8`, and `2.1 t/24 h` figures are supplier references from specific test conditions and are not guarantees. A quotation and FAT/SAT protocol should state the raw-material species and lot, pretreatment, particle-size distribution, feed moisture, batch mass, the complete loading-to-cleaning-and-restart cycle, continuous test duration, permitted stops, weighing and calibration method, sampling or laboratory method, acceptance tolerance, utilities, and operator tasks. Only the criteria written into the signed contract form the acceptance boundary.
