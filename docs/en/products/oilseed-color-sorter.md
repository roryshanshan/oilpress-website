# Color Sorter for Peanuts, Sesame and Oilseeds, 0.5–10 t/h

![Seven-chute oilseed color sorter 6SXZ-448 with touch screen](/images/color-sorter/oilseed-color-sorter-6sxz-448a.webp)

A CCD camera color sorter looks at every kernel and seed as it falls down the chute and blows out the bad ones with a jet of air: moldy, black, shriveled or discolored kernels, stones, clods and husk that the screen and destoner let through. It is the last cleaning step before roasting or pressing: for peanut oil and peanut butter it takes out the moldy kernels that carry aflatoxin; for sesame it lifts black and immature seeds, stones and shells.

## What It Removes

![Peanut, cashew, walnut and hazelnut kernels for color sorting](/images/color-sorter/nut-kernels-for-color-sorting.webp)

- **Peanuts and nut kernels:** moldy, black, shriveled, sprouted and insect-damaged kernels, kernels with red skin left after peeling, and stray shell. The same machine grades cashews, almonds, walnuts and hazelnuts.
- **Sesame:** black, moldy and immature seeds, stones, clods and seeds still in the husk.
- **Beans and grains:** soybeans, mung beans, corn, wheat, millet and rice run on the same machine with a different preset program.

## How It Works

- **Feeding:** a vibrating feeder spreads the material over the chutes in a single, even layer.
- **Seeing:** full-color high-speed CCD cameras under shadow-free LED light pick out spots down to 0.04 mm² and judge both color and shape.
- **Rejecting:** high-frequency solenoid ejectors blow each bad kernel into the reject hopper; good kernels fall straight through.
- **Setting:** the touch screen stores a preset program for each crop, so changing from peanuts to sesame is a matter of choosing a program.

## Before You Order

- **Clean first, then sort.** Run the seed through a screen and destoner before the sorter; dust, straw and a heavy stone load slow it down and waste air.
- **Size it to your line.** A one-chute 6SXZ-64 suits a small press workshop; work out tonnes per hour from daily intake and working hours, then add a margin.
- **Plan the compressed air.** Every model needs at least 0.6 MPa at the air flow in the table, with a dryer and filter. Ask us to quote the compressor with the sorter.
- **Send a sample.** A few kilograms of your real seed lets us test, set the program and confirm capacity and reject rate before you order.

## FAQ

### Does color sorting lower aflatoxin in peanut oil?

Aflatoxin is concentrated in moldy, discolored and damaged kernels, which are what the sorter rejects. Sorting cuts the aflatoxin load before pressing, but it does not replace laboratory testing against your market's limit.

### Can one machine sort both peanuts and sesame?

Yes. The chutes take both and the touch screen stores a program for each crop. Clean the chutes and hoppers when you change crops.

### Do I need a color sorter for a small oil press?

Not always. If you buy clean, graded seed, a screen is enough. A sorter pays when your seed comes in mixed, when you sell bottled oil or paste under your own brand, or when buyers test for aflatoxin.
