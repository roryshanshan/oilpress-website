## 426 Material Route: Match Barrel Diameter to the Material

The published 426 configurations provide several barrel diameters and single- or dual-barrel options. Use that flexibility to match bulk density, loading behavior, drainage and cycle time; do not assume one barrel fits every material or that a larger diameter guarantees more recovered oil.

### Cold-press examples

- **Perilla seed:** clean, stabilize moisture, crush with controlled heat and press directly.
- **Hemp seed:** verify seed cleaning, storage condition, particle size and oxidation control.
- **Almond:** record kernel grade, moisture and crushing uniformity before the same-lot trial.
- **Pumpkin seed:** state hull condition and agree how net filtered oil and cake residual oil will be measured.

![Perilla seed preparation for direct cold pressing](/images/an-overview-of-the-cold-pressing-process-of-perill-76371f15c4.webp)

![Pumpkin seed preparation for direct cold pressing](/images/an-overview-of-the-cold-pressing-process-of-pumpki-c206ee35aa.webp)

### Changeover and acceptance

For a multi-material line, the quotation should define barrel choice, cleaning access, cross-contact controls, crusher screen changes, filtration consumables and storage segregation. Run repeatable trials for each material family instead of transferring results from walnut, sesame or another seed.

### Industrial configuration

Spent bleaching clay and activated-carbon decolorizer must be assessed as industrial feed. Require SDS review, closed liquid collection, explosion and exposure controls where applicable, and a written waste-handling boundary.

## Delivery and After-sales Scope

### Before Shipment
- Confirm the machine configuration, voltage, accessories and packing list in the quotation.
- Request the final drawing, operating manual and available test records for the supplied model.
- Agree on installation, commissioning and operator-guidance responsibilities before the order is released.

### Installation and Operation
- Follow the supplied manual for hydraulic oil filling, electrical checks, rotation checks and the first no-load movement.
- Record the agreed acceptance material, batch load, cycle conditions and observable oil/cake results during commissioning.
- Remote guidance or on-site service, where required, is quoted according to the destination and project scope.

### Spare Parts and Technical Questions
- Identify replacement parts by machine model, drawing reference and component photo.
- Confirm price, availability and delivery time for each spare-parts request.
- Submit operating data, pressure readings, photos or video through the [contact page](/en/contact/) for diagnosis.
