## Direct Cold-Press Trial and Acceptance Record

The 355-500 series is positioned for direct hydraulic cold pressing after cleaning and crushing. Do not use a generic oil-yield table: validate the selected model with the buyer's actual material and agreed finished-oil target.

| Trial field | Record before acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Raw material route | Walnut, sea buckthorn seed, perilla, coconut, tea seed, hemp, flaxseed, almond, pine nut or pumpkin seed; identify the exact lot |
| Feed condition | Cleaning result, measured moisture, low-temperature drying when required, particle-size range and feed temperature |
| Machine setup | Basket diameter, actual load, pressure sequence, holding time, unloading time and complete cycle time |
| Oil result | Net oil after the agreed settling or filtration step, with temperature and container tare recorded |
| Cake result | Cake mass, condition and residual oil measured by the agreed laboratory method |
| Acceptance basis | Repeatable same-lot trials, agreed sampling, measuring instruments and written acceptance limits |

Industrial materials such as waste bleaching clay or activated-carbon decolorizer require a separate SDS review, closed collection, worker protection and disposal plan. They are not part of a food-oil cold-press acceptance test.

## 355 Material Route: Prove the Cold-Press Recipe

Use the 355 series when a project is moving from material trials into repeatable small- or medium-batch cold pressing. The baseline route is **screening -> cleaning -> optional low-temperature drying -> high-speed crushing -> direct hydraulic pressing -> settling or filtration**. Roasting is not part of this route.

### Best starting trials

- **Walnut and almond:** control kernel condition, oxidation exposure, moisture and particle size before loading.
- **Perilla and flaxseed:** verify cleaning, fine-particle handling, crusher heat and filtration media.
- **Sea buckthorn seed:** confirm separation from pulp, drying history and the actual oil-bearing fraction.
- **Pumpkin seed and pine nut:** state whether hulls are removed and record kernel damage during preparation.

![Walnut direct cold-press preparation](/images/process-image-647a0e9868.webp)

![Sea buckthorn seed cold-press preparation](/images/cold-pressing-process-of-sea-buckthorn-seeds-7674903603.webp)

### What the 355 quotation should settle

Confirm single-, dual-, or mother-child barrel configuration; actual batch load; crusher capacity; filtration method; cleaning between materials; and the same-lot acceptance record. A larger barrel is selected through trial load, drainage, cycle time and cake residual oil, not through a promised yield increase.

### Industrial material boundary

Waste bleaching clay and activated-carbon decolorizer require an SDS, solvent and volatile-content review, closed collection, worker protection and a disposal route. Keep this work separate from food-oil production and request a dedicated trial and quotation.
