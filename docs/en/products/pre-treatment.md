# Oil Seeds Pre-treatment Equipment

Pre-treatment is not one fixed machine package. The correct scope depends on the raw material, incoming impurity level, moisture, required particle size, hot- or cold-press route, press loading rhythm, and the finished-oil specification.

## Select the Route Before the Equipment

- **Small dry seeds:** screen dust, oversize material, stones and metal before pressing. Start with the [sesame screening machine](/en/products/sesame-screening-machine) when the material is sesame, rapeseed, flaxseed, perilla or another small seed.
- **Hard kernels and nuts:** confirm shell removal, grading and the particle size required by the selected press. A sample test should define whether the [11 kW pulverizer](/en/products/11kw-high-speed-pulverizer) or another crusher is suitable.
- **300/325 hot-press projects:** cleaning and crushing are followed by controlled steaming, conditioning or roasting. Compare the [27 kW electric steamer](/en/products/27kw-electric-steamer), [wood-fired steamer](/en/products/wood-fired-steamer) and [automatic drum roaster](/en/products/automatic-drum-roaster) against the actual batch size and heat source.
- **355-500 cold-press projects:** clean the material, use low-temperature drying only when required, then crush and load it directly into the hydraulic pressing barrel. Do not add roasting or high-temperature steaming to a cold-process quotation unless the buyer requests a different route.
- **Fresh fruit, palm fruit, avocado and rice bran:** these materials need a project-specific process review and must not be treated as ordinary dry oilseeds.

## Typical Pre-treatment Modules

| Module | Purchase decision |
| --- | --- |
| Screening, aspiration and stone removal | Confirm feed cleanliness, screen opening, dust outlet and metal-removal requirement |
| Dehulling and separation | Confirm whether hull removal is required and how kernels, hulls and fines are collected |
| Crushing, flaking or grinding | Confirm target particle range, temperature rise, throughput and cleaning between materials |
| Steaming, conditioning or roasting | Confirm hot-process recipe, heat source, batch size, temperature measurement and discharge method |
| Conveying and buffering | Match the real capacity of each module to the press cycle, not only the nameplate throughput |

The [feeding, crushing and frying integrated machine](/en/products/feeding-crushing-frying-integrated-machine) and [pre-press machine](/en/products/pre-press-machine) are optional process modules, not universal requirements.

## Information Required for a Factory Quote

1. Raw-material name, photos or sample, impurity level, moisture and whether shells are present.
2. Planned hot-press or cold-press route and the required finished-oil stage.
3. Target batch, shift or daily production and expected material-change frequency.
4. Selected press model, available floor space, voltage, heat source, dust extraction and local utility limits.
5. Required scope: individual machine, connected line, controls, installation support, spare parts and documentation.

## Acceptance Basis

Use the buyer's representative material to record feed condition, cleaned loss, particle-size setting, temperature where applicable, actual throughput, transfer stability, cleaning access and safety interlocks. Oil yield and oil quality remain results of the complete material and process route; they are not guaranteed by purchasing a pre-treatment machine alone.
