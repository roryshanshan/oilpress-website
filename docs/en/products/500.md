## Direct Cold-Press Trial and Acceptance Record

The 355-500 series is positioned for direct hydraulic cold pressing after cleaning and crushing. Do not use a generic oil-yield table: validate the selected model with the buyer's actual material and agreed finished-oil target.

| Trial field | Record before acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Raw material route | Walnut, sea buckthorn seed, perilla, coconut, tea seed, hemp, flaxseed, almond, pine nut or pumpkin seed; identify the exact lot |
| Feed condition | Cleaning result, measured moisture, low-temperature drying when required, particle-size range and feed temperature |
| Machine setup | Basket diameter, actual load, pressure sequence, holding time, unloading time and complete cycle time |
| Oil result | Net oil after the agreed settling or filtration step, with temperature and container tare recorded |
| Cake result | Cake mass, condition and residual oil measured by the agreed laboratory method |
| Acceptance basis | Repeatable same-lot trials, agreed sampling, measuring instruments and written acceptance limits |

Industrial materials such as waste bleaching clay or activated-carbon decolorizer require a separate SDS review, closed collection, worker protection and disposal plan. They are not part of a food-oil cold-press acceptance test.

## 500 Material Route: Define High-Load Project Acceptance

The 500 is the highest-force model in the published 355-500 series. It should be selected only after the buyer has confirmed the material route, actual batch requirement, site handling and the economics of one large press versus multiple smaller presses. The cold-press route remains **clean -> condition moisture if required -> crush -> direct barrel pressing -> controlled settling or filtration**.

### Materials for witnessed trials

- **Pine nut and walnut:** record kernel quality, oxidation exposure, moisture, crushing and net filtered oil.
- **Pumpkin seed and almond:** state hull condition, batch load, cake discharge and cleaning time.
- **Hemp, flaxseed and perilla:** control dust, fine-particle loss, crusher temperature and filtration endpoint.
- **Tea seed and coconut:** define feed preparation and moisture limits before confirming barrel and cycle settings.

![Pumpkin seed material for direct cold pressing](/images/an-overview-of-the-cold-pressing-process-of-pumpki-c206ee35aa.webp)

![Flaxseed preparation for direct hydraulic pressing](/images/an-overview-of-the-cold-pressing-process-of-flaxse-0ea1b504ce.webp)

### Contract and delivery gates

Before ordering, confirm machine and barrel configuration, foundation and lifting conditions, utilities, upstream crusher output, downstream filtration and storage, spare parts, export packing, installation responsibility and acceptance remedies. For multi-machine projects, demonstrate the planned staggered cycle and operator workload rather than multiplying a nominal batch figure.

### Industrial-residue projects

Chemical waste clay and activated-carbon decolorizer need a separate engineering review based on the SDS and representative sample. The contract should define containment, worker protection, cleaning, collected liquid handling and lawful residue disposal.

## Delivery and After-sales Scope

### Before Shipment
- Confirm the machine configuration, voltage, accessories and packing list in the quotation.
- Request the final drawing, operating manual and available test records for the supplied model.
- Agree on installation, commissioning and operator-guidance responsibilities before the order is released.

### Installation and Operation
- Follow the supplied manual for hydraulic oil filling, electrical checks, rotation checks and the first no-load movement.
- Record the agreed acceptance material, batch load, cycle conditions and observable oil/cake results during commissioning.
